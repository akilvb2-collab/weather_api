# Weather basics

Relative humidity is the percentage of water vapor in the air compared with the maximum amount the air can hold at its current temperature. High humidity can make warm weather feel hotter.

Weather code 0 means clear sky. Codes 1, 2, and 3 describe increasing cloud cover. Codes 51 through 57 describe drizzle, codes 61 through 67 describe rain, and codes 71 through 77 describe snow.
